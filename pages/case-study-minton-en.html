UI/UX Design Case Study

Minton - Badminton Ecosystem Website

Client / Company

Minton (via VINIX)

Role

UI/UX Designer

Focus

Ecosystem & Community Platform

Category

Web Platform

Project Overview

Minton is a badminton ecosystem platform designed to bring the community together, provide court booking services, and facilitate tournament registration. The project aims to create an interface that sparks sports enthusiasm while simplifying administrative processes for both players and court owners.

Design Challenge

The biggest challenge was building a tournament registration and court booking flow that is intuitive for users of various ages. The design also needed to radiate an energetic and dynamic feel typical of sports without compromising the accessibility of the main core functionalities.

Solution & Approach

Utilizing bold typography and a spacious layout to highlight key features. The booking process is simplified through a step-by-step interface that provides clear visual guidance. The visual design uses sporty elements with an information hierarchy that guides users to their goals quickly.
