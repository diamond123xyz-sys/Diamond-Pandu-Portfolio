Project Overview

During my internship at PT VINIX Seven Aurum, I led two main projects involving dual roles as a UI/UX Designer and Front-End Developer. The first project was Indigo by Telkom – AI Synergy & Risk Engine, an artificial intelligence-based risk analysis dashboard designed to help decision-makers understand risk data visually and intuitively. The second project was Minton, a badminton ecosystem platform combining court booking features, tournament registration, and player community into a single unified interface.

Design Challenge

Each project presented unique challenges — Indigo required complex AI data visualization that remained easily understandable for decision-makers, with a clear information hierarchy and efficient navigation across risk analytics modules. Meanwhile, Minton demanded an intuitive booking flow for users of various ages, while reflecting the energetic and dynamic branding of badminton without compromising on the accessibility of key features.

Outcomes & Impact

Both projects were successfully deployed live — Indigo on Vercel as a production-ready web app, and Minton on GitHub Pages as a fully-responsive platform. The entire process was handled end-to-end, ranging from user research, wireframing, high-fidelity prototyping in Figma, to Front-End implementation using vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
