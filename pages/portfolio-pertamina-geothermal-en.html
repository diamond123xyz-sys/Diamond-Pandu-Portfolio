In developing the PROPER presentation materials for PT Pertamina Geothermal Energy Tbk Lahendong Area, I explored a design style that specifically adopts the theme of social innovation in the green energy-based agricultural sector. I applied a curated color palette: Green to represent the eco-friendly geothermal ecosystem, Yellow as a symbol of energy, growth, and optimism for the fostered farmers, and White to convey cleanliness and data transparency.

This visual approach aims to bridge geothermal technology's identity with the tangible impact of community empowerment around the Lahendong operational area. By blending technical agricultural infographics and modern layouts, I successfully created a harmonious visual narrative to present the company's contribution in generating economic independence to the Ministry of Environment and Forestry of the Republic of Indonesia.
